Enforced Library Business Rules

  • Full Name Required: Single-word names are rejected; enter first and last name separated by a space.
  • Verified Email Providers: Only valid @gmail.com, @yahoo.com, @outlook.com, or .edu.ph emails are accepted.
  • No Past Dates & 14-Day Cap: Borrow date cannot be in the past, and due date must be within 14 calendar days.
  • Real-Time Stock & Loan Limits: Out-of-stock books cannot be borrowed, and each ID is limited to 3 active books (no duplicate titles).
